About Chatter

Chatter is a multi-functional platform where readers can have access to their own content. It aims to be a traditional bookworm's heaven ad a blog to get access to more text based content. Our vision is to foster an inclusive and vibrant community where diversity is celebrated. We encourage open-mindedness and respect for all individuals, regardless of their backgrounds or beliefs. By dialog and understanding we strive.

Why you should join chatter

Our goals is to make writers and readers see our platform as their next heaven for blogging, ensuring ease in interactions, connecting with like-minded peers, have access to favorite content based on interests and able to communicate your great ideas with people.

Analytics

Analytics to track the number of views, likes and comment and also analyze the performance of your articles over time.

Social interactions

Users on the platform can interact with posts they like, comment and engage in discussion

Content creation

Write nice and appealing with our in-built markdown, a rich text editor
